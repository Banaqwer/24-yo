Gaps raised:
- Homebuilders are absent. Closings (the quarter's revenue) and starts (next quarter's deliveries) are recorded in county permit, certificate-of-occupancy and deed files under the builders' subsidiary LLC names within days of the event. Zonda and John Burns sell field-survey starts and closings at scale, but the county exhaust is cheaper, daily and lot-level, and sell-side anchors on the builders' own order disclosures (REASONED). Lead time: quarter-end CO counts are final 3-4 weeks before the print. (regulatory, Consumer discretionary (homebuilding), building products): Reuse N51's permit pipeline: Austin Socrata 3syk-w9eu (applicant/contractor fields, work_class, issue_date, final-inspection dates) and LA pi9x-tg5x are already TESTED; add UNPROBED portals for Maricopa/Phoenix, Harris/Houston, Clark NV, Hillsborough and Orange FL, Wake/Mecklenburg NC, Dallas/Fort Worth, San Antonio (the markets that dominate the mid-cap builders). Census Building Permits Survey place-level monthly (census.gov/construction/bps, keyless, UNPROBED) as control; Census/HUD manufactured-housing shipments for SKY/CVCO.
- Generic and injectable pharma (AMPH, ANIP, AMRX, HROW and peers) and specialty pharma have no idea. A competitor's entry onto or exit from the FDA drug-shortage list, a first-generic ANDA approval, or the quarterly CMS ASP reimbursement file changes the quarter's volume and price; all are scheduled, public and product-specific. IQVIA weekly scripts, the consensus input, cover hospital injectables poorly (REASONED). (regulatory, Healthcare ex-biotech (generic and specialty pharma)): openFDA drug-shortages endpoint api.fda.gov/drug/shortages.json (UNPROBED; openFDA device endpoints are DATA in the working file, so the host is reachable); Drugs@FDA weekly data files (fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/drugsfda-data-files, UNPROBED); FDA first-generic approvals list; CMS Part B ASP quarterly pricing files (cms.gov Medicare Part B drug ASP files, keyless xlsx published ~6-8 weeks before the effective quarter, UNPROBED). ASHP shortage list terms UNKNOWN; accessdata.fda.gov pages bounced the container in the N23 probe, so prefer api.fda.gov and fda.gov static files.
- Upstream E&P is uncovered and N53's only source (FracFocus) is blocked. Operator-level completion reports, drilling permits and monthly production filed with state regulators give turn-in-line counts for the quarter, the main driver of production surprises, and Texas RRC completion reports carry the same frac job data as FracFocus for Texas wells (a bypass for N53). Enverus and Novi sell this at scale, but the regulator files are free and same-day for permits and completions (REASONED); E&Ps often pre-release volumes mid-quarter, so the gap must be measured against those updates. (physical, Energy (E&P and oilfield services)): Texas RRC data sets for download (rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/data-sets-available-for-download: Drilling Permit Master, Oil & Gas Completions, Production Data Query dumps; UNPROBED); New Mexico OCD permits and completions (ocdimage.emnrd.nm.gov and the OCD data portal, UNPROBED); North Dakota DMR monthly production by well and operator plus Director's Cut (dmr.nd.gov/oilgas, ~6-week lag, UNPROBED). EIA API v2 (KEY, in working file) for EIA-914 state production as an aggregate control.
- No idea measures gas throughput at single plants. FERC-mandated informational postings on interstate pipelines publish daily scheduled quantities at every receipt and delivery point; for a single-plant nitrogen producer, gas-fed chemical site, gas processor or LNG exporter this is a daily physical run-rate nowcast that complements N03/S07 (effluent, CEMS). Wood Mackenzie/Genscape aggregate these for energy desks (crowded there), but plant-level delivery series for mid-cap chemicals and fertilizer names are not in sell-side models (REASONED). (physical, Energy and materials (fertilizer, gas-fed chemicals, midstream processors, LNG)): Each pipeline's Informational Postings > Capacity > Scheduled Quantity / Operationally Available Capacity pages, public under 18 CFR 284.13 and NAESB WGQ standards (e.g., Williams Transco 1Line, Kinder Morgan Tennessee and El Paso, Boardwalk Gulf South, Energy Transfer Trunkline); UNPROBED from the container. Map points to plants with the EPA ECHO facility service (DATA) and the EIA-757 natural gas processing plant list (EIA API v2, KEY).
- Trade remedies are missing from the regulatory lens. Commerce AD/CVD preliminary and final determinations, Section 232/301 inclusion and exclusion decisions and CBP implementation messages are scheduled, public, product- and exporter-specific, and change the reported quarter's import competition and pricing for domestic producers of steel products, aluminum extrusions, cabinets, tires, lumber and chemicals (IIIN, NWPI, CENX, KALU, CSTM, ZEUS, RYI, TMST, AMWD, MBC, JELD). Direction is in consensus once the schedule is known; the duty-rate magnitude and the Census import unit-value response in the quarter are not (REASONED). (regulatory, Materials and industrials (metals, building products, chemicals)): Federal Register API (federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json, keyless; filter agencies=international-trade-administration, type=NOTICE, term 'antidumping'; UNPROBED); Commerce ITA ACCESS case calendar (access.trade.gov, UNPROBED); USITC EDIS; CBP CSMS message feed (content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP, UNPROBED).
