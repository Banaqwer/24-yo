# Grade A: what it honestly is and how to improve it

Desk note, 2026-11-11, revision 2 (after three independent checks: statistical, leakage/definition, desk review, and two independent re-verifications of the revised text; the register of what changed, what was rejected and the final wording corrections is section 12). All numbers are traceable to files under `research/lab/grade_a/` (named per table). Plain ASCII; every interval is a 90% ISO-week block-bootstrap CI (2,000 draws, numpy default_rng(0)) unless marked 80%; dates are ISO.

## 0. Scope

Grade A is the Asia-session engine's top call: at 17:xx ET on RTH date D, `confidence_from_plan` (model.py L513-562) picks the max-EV candidate (fade or continuation; tight or wide stop; VWAP / 38.2% / 50% retrace targets, or the fade's stop level for a continuation) among the candidates passing the geometry guard, and grades it A when confidence = round(100 x p_win/(p_win+p_loss)) >= 72. Window: the out-of-sample record 2025-04-01..2026-11-06 (NQ 331 nights, ES 301), split into discovery 2025-04-01..2026-05-31 (14.0 months) and vault 2026-06-01..2026-11-06 (5.2 months); 19.2 months in all. The honest baseline (`trades_honest_{SYM}.csv`, column `pnl_net_open`) is the published engine with the integrity defects removed: causal monthly calibrator (H0a), walk-forward range/EV regressors (H0b), own-pair raw-bar resolution with one entry reference, stop priority and gap fills (H0c/H0j/H0k), fill at the 18:00 open with unfillable nights booked at 0 (H0d), and costs (H0h). Costs: NQ 1.5 pts per round trip on weeknights, 4.0 on Sunday reopens and on nights with |post_ret| > 0.3% or a mega-cap print; ES 0.75 / 2.0; sensitivity line 4.0 / 2.0 everywhere (`pnl_net_open_hicost`). Dollars: NQ USD 20 per point, ES USD 50; per contract per year = points x 12 / months in the split (19.2 full, 14.0 discovery, 5.2 vault) x point value; every dollar effect below is a point estimate with its 90% CI, and per-rule dollar figures are each measured against the baseline alone and are not additive. The four contract-splice nights (NQ 2025-09-16, 2025-09-17, 2025-12-16, 2025-12-17; ES three of them) are flagged and excluded from every statistic.

Three definitions the first draft left implicit. (1) The record holds the 331 RTH dates on which the engine's 17:xx-knowable mask (|rth_ret| >= 0.15%) passes AND `data/NQ_1h.csv` has Asia bars for the following session: of the 398 RTH dates in the window, 59 fail the mask and 8 have no Asia bars in the feed. Two of those eight are closed sessions (2025-04-17 before Good Friday, 2025-12-31), but four are Friday decisions with |rth_ret| >= 0.15% whose Sunday 18:00 reopen existed and is simply missing from the feed (2025-07-11, 2025-08-29, 2026-01-16, 2026-05-01, i.e. the sessions of 2025-07-13, 2025-08-31, 2026-01-18, 2026-05-03; 2026-05-22 and 2026-09-04 likewise but below the mask): the engine would have issued a call on them, their outcomes are unknown, and they are absent from every Sunday statistic (H8). The forward resolver must log a missing-session night as unresolved, never drop it (LAB_DESIGN H0f/H0i). (2) `pre_holiday` (calendar_features.py L157-174) is 1 only when the next CALENDAR day is a US holiday: the next-trading-day branch is dead code, so a Friday before a Monday holiday is not flagged (2025-05-23 carries pre_holiday 0). No OOS night's statistic is affected (the other such Fridays are the missing Sunday sessions above), but an operator implementing "next trading day" would veto nights the backtest did not; the next-trading-day semantics, if wanted, is a registered change that touches no OOS night. (3) The H3 wide-fade floor is applied to the raw ratio p_win/(p_win+p_loss) >= 0.80, while LAB_DESIGN 8 #4 words it as "conf >= 80" (the rounded confidence); the two differ on candidates with ratio in [0.795, 0.80): there are 2 of 882 wide-fade candidates (2025-06-25, 2025-10-14), neither the chosen candidate of its night, so no night's call changes under either convention (`h_geometry_results.json`, `H3_floor_convention`).

### 1(c) Verdict

What the baseline is. Grade A as published (+21.3 pts/night, 69% win, USD 20.8k per contract per year) does not exist. The pooled isotonic calibrator graded nights with knowledge of later outcomes; with a causal calibrator the same engine grades 86 nights A and averages -0.05. The walk-forward regressors, a tradeable fill, honest resolution and costs leave an honest Grade A of 84 nights at +6.7 pts/night (56% win, CI [-8.7, +21.9], worst -243, USD 7.0k/yr [-9.1k, +23.0k]), carried by 14 Sunday reopens (66% of the points) and the 90+ confidence band (21 nights, +552 of the +560 total); on discovery alone it is +3.5, below the reset alarm, and +0.1 without the four raw-probability months. ES is not tradeable after costs at any grade.

What is supported on the vault. Nothing passes the shop's vault rule: the four groups scored 41 tests on Vault-H and none reaches the 12.3 slot alpha of 0.0083 (the smallest one-sided p is 0.116, H2; H3's is 0.41), so Vault-H is spent and every later test is Vault-F. Two free vetoes carry the vault evidence under the rule that applies to them (7.3: a no-harm criterion, met on both splits): the after-hours-print veto H5 and the pre-holiday skip H9. Applied to the honest engine they are Grade A prime (spec S6 in `grade_a_prime.py`): 73 A nights at +16.5 (62% win, sd 79, 0.21 pts per sigma) on the full window, paired all-night effect +1.97 pts/night [+0.44, +3.97] = USD +8,070 [+1,790, +16,215] per contract per year (+8,132 at the high-cost line); on the vault A 22 -> 17 at +43.3 (71% win, worst -138), A-average effect +27.6 [+2.7, +62.3] with random-filter percentile 99.2, but a paired all-night effect of +4.08 [-0.07, +10.05] = USD +18,059 [-306, +44,513] that does not exclude zero on 96 nights, and a vault one-sided p of 0.128 on the family statistic. It is paper-traded at one contract as a PASS*-grade integration of two free rules, not as a confirmed edge: its deflated Sharpe on the full window is 0.20 at 122 trials (0.23 at 90, 0.15 at 250) against the 0.95 gate.

What is not supported. The geometry layer is discovery-only. H3 (prune wide continuation and sub-0.80 wide fades) has a full-window A-average effect of +11.1 [+0.5, +22.3] and a graded-book effect that is sign-consistent on discovery, but on the vault it is a null (p 0.41, random-filter 83/63, same sign only) and its A-book sign is positive in 1 of 4 discovery quarters; it is logged nightly as the shadow spec S7 (H3 + H5 + H9: 54 A nights at +24.2 full, +2.29/night [-0.24, +5.17] over all nights; vault +2.24 [-3.80, +9.23]) and earns a forward band, not a trade. H2 (target cap at 1.0 x med20) is dead under LAB_DESIGN 2.6: a cap at 1.0 x any night's med20 removes the same large-target candidates (placebo median 104% of the discovery effect, 97% of the full effect), so what it measured was an absolute target-size cap, which may be re-registered under a new id. Inside the first draft's four-rule spec, H2 changed 4 nights in 19.2 months (three discovery nights net -8.3, one vault night +137.8) and was the only reason that spec's vault A-average CI excluded zero (+42.5 [+9.7, +78.3] with it, +27.4 [-9.3, +68.0] without); the geometry layer's paired increment over the free vetoes is +1.17 [-0.86, +3.61] on discovery, -0.40 [-4.19, +3.67] on the vault and +0.71 [-1.19, +2.74] on the full window. Everything else tested here is unproven or dead: no entry-timing or exit rule, no detector checkpoint, no cross-asset filter, no gate and no second-stage calibration survives on the honest record at this sample size. The honest vault holds 22 A nights before and 17 after the free vetoes; nothing A-specific can be confirmed on that many nights, and the forward track is the only place either spec can earn its label.

## 2. Method

**Honest baseline.** `honest_baseline.py` replays the committed engine (`Engine(sym).train()`, `backtest.simulate` reproduces the committed CSV to 3e-14) and removes one defect per step. H0a: the isotonic calibrator is refitted on the first day of each month on OOS predictions strictly before the month (min 60 rows; raw probabilities before that, i.e. months 2025-04..07; first causal fit 2025-08 on 63 rows). H0b: rng_q50 / rng_q80 / exp_rev are refitted on the first day of each month on all sample rows strictly before the month (the 168-night training prefix plus the prior OOS rows: n_train 284 in 2025-11, 460 in 2026-09); the exp_rev shrink is the R^2 of the walk-forward exp_rev predictions on the prior OOS rows, clipped to [0, 1], and is 0 in every month because that R^2 is negative in every month (-0.03..-0.10), so the scratch valuation never enters the EV, exactly as in v0. H0c/H0j/H0k: each candidate's own (target, stop) pair is walked on the raw hourly bars from one entry reference; stop priority inside a bar; a bar opening beyond the stop fills at its open; targets fill at level; scratch = last Asia close minus the fill. H0d: fill at the 18:00 open; a night whose open is already beyond the target (already_past_open) or the stop (open_past_stop) is not traded and books 0 (5 honest A nights: 2025-08-08, 2026-04-17, 2026-10-09 past the target; 2025-05-28, 2026-06-03 past the stop). H0h: costs as in section 0. Resolution self-check: 128 of 196 v0 graded NQ nights agree to 1e-6; the 68 that differ are all explained (60 scratch-reference nights, 7 stop-through gap fills, 1 same-bar continuation booked by v0 as a win) (`baseline_summary.json`, `self_checks`). The engine's classifiers, the causal calibrator and the walk-forward regressors are trained on all prior sample rows including the four splice nights (inherited from the committed engine); "excluded from every statistic" is true of the statistics only.

**Splits and protocol.** Every rule carries one pre-registered parameter (LAB_DESIGN section 8) and is sized on discovery, then scored once on the vault; discovery, vault and full are reported separately; where a group explored a grid it did so on discovery only, labelled exploratory, and scored the vault only at the pre-registered value. NQ is primary; ES is a same-sign replication arm read by LAB_DESIGN 12.4 (supports / contradicts only when its own 80% CI excludes zero).

**Statistics.** Paired per-night differences (hypothesis minus baseline) with an ISO-week block bootstrap (2,000 draws, default_rng(0)) 90% CI (80% from the same draws where quoted), on four populations: the A book over all nights (non-A = 0), the changed nights, the A-either nights, and the graded book. ## 9. Operating procedure (Grade A prime, NQ only, one contract)

1. 17:05-17:20 ET, decision for the Asia session 18:00 D .. 02:59 D+1 (on a Friday, for the Sunday 18:00 reopen). Run the honest engine (monthly retrain; causal calibrator refit on the first trading day of each month on prior OOS months only; walk-forward range regressors refit on all sample rows before the month). Build the candidate table; choose the max-EV guard passer; grade A >= 72, B >= 63, C >= 56, else NO EDGE. No geometry change to the traded call. In parallel, compute and log the shadow call S7: drop every CONTINUATION|wide candidate and every MEAN REVERSION|wide candidate whose raw ratio p_win/(p_win+p_loss) < 0.80, re-choose the max-EV guard passer and re-grade; the shadow is logged, never traded.
2. Vetoes on the traded call (and on the shadow call), in this order, each logged with its reason: |post_ret| > 0.3% (post_close / rth_close - 1, from the 16:00-17:00 bar, on splice-checked bars) or megacap_earnings_tonight = 1 -> NO EDGE (H5); pre_holiday = 1 -> NO EDGE (H9, exactly as scored: the next calendar day is a US holiday; a Friday before a Monday holiday is NOT flagged unless the next-trading-day semantics is registered as a change first; `holiday_tomorrow` is not part of the rule).
3. Detector check (logged, not acted on): record pB_high_above_cal and pB_low_below_cal (17:xx) and the D1 condition; record pA_high_k / pA_low_k and the overnight flags at every bar close through the night. These feed the forward bands of D1 and D2_switch; no position is changed by them. The log is decision-time honest only if the detector panels are produced before 17:50 ET in production.
4. 18:00 ET fill: enter at the first Asia print (the 18:00 open; on Sunday the 18:00 reopen with the 4-pt cost). If the open is already beyond the target (already_past_open) or beyond the stop (open_past_stop), stand down and log 0. The target and stop are the registered levels from the 17:00 post_close (tight stop = RTH extreme + 10% of the RTH range; targets VWAP / 38.2% / 50% retrace; continuation target = the fade's stop level, stop = VWAP).
5. No checkpoint, no time-stop, no breakeven move, no partial: hold the registered target and stop to the 02:59 close; a stop-through gap fills at that bar's open; flat at the last Asia close if neither level is hit; never hold into Europe.
6. 03:10 ET resolver: book gross and net P&L at the base and the high-cost line for Grade A prime (S6) and, side by side, for the honest baseline call (spec_v0.1), the shadow S7 and every forward-band rule (D1, D2_switch, TS22c, the ES no-call veto, H8) so the paired forward test of LAB_DESIGN 12.3 item 1 can be computed from the log. A night whose Asia bars are missing from the feed is logged as unresolved, not dropped (section 0). Log per night: date, call, grade, confidence, cand_id, entry / target / stop, target_dist, stop_dist, med20_pts, post_ret, megacap flag, pre_holiday, holiday_tomorrow, pB_dir, the pA_* path, fill price, exit price, outcome, resolved_k, cost, P&L, the veto reason, the shadow call and its P&L, and the baseline counterfactual.
7. Before it is traded at size. Paper or one contract. The forward conditions in reachable units, at this spec's full-window rate of 3.8 A nights per month (73 in 19.2 months) and 17.0 nights per month: (a) >= 80 forward nights with the all-night paired (S6 minus v0.1) 90% lower bound > 0 (LAB_DESIGN 1.2 3(a); the paired sd of 45-70 pts makes +8 pts/night detectable at n=80) is reachable at ~4.7 months; (b) >= 25 forward A nights with net expectancy >= +10 and a trimmed mean > 0 takes ~6.6 months, just beyond the 6-month forward cap; (c) pooled ECE <= 10 on >= 60 resolved graded nights; (d) >= 2.5 A nights per month (discovery under the spec: 4.0). A 40-A-night statistic needs ~10.5 months. So inside the forward phase the operative kill switches are the trailing 12 Grade-A forward nights with net sum < -470 pts and a pooled ECE > 15 on the trailing 40 resolved graded nights (LAB_DESIGN 12.3 item 5); the rolling-40-night expectancy rule cannot be evaluated before ~10 months, and there is no size-up inside the forward phase. For calibration of the trailing-12 rule: under S6 the honest record breaches -470 in 9 of 62 trailing-12 windows (worst -693, the window 2025-10-02..2025-11-24), the honest baseline in 12 of 73 (worst -865), the shadow S7 never (worst -391). Per element: H5 flags ~1 graded night per month and H9 ~0.15, so both stay free rules with the no-harm criterion checked monthly (H5 retained only if the forward flagged nights average below zero over ~60 nights). The shadow's H3 touches ~6 graded nights per month (118 affected in 19.2 months), so its 25-affected-night band is reachable in ~4 months; a bump of the paper-traded spec to S7 is allowed at most once, at the start of forward month 1 or 2 (12.3 item 2), only if that band holds and the ES vault contradiction has not repeated. Kill: demote to research-only and post-mortem. Re-open: after >= 40 forward nights, a paired 80% lower bound < 0 over the trailing 60 nights.
8. Capacity and fills. Everything above is measured at one contract with an assumed cost schedule (1.5 pts per round trip on weeknights, 4.0 on Sunday reopens and print nights) that has not been measured against live prints; the 18:00 ET first print and the Sunday 18:00 reopen are the thinnest prints of the session, the 15-minute midpoint fill (H0k) was not run, and 14 of the 73 Grade A prime nights are Sunday reopens whose levels were registered 49 hours earlier (the worst night of the spec, -243 on 2025-11-14, is one of them). The forward phase runs at one contract; before any size above one contract on NQ a measured slippage study of the 18:00 print and the Sunday reopen is required, and Sunday half-size is registered as a sizing hypothesis under LAB_DESIGN 7.1 (registrable only if it improves pts per sigma by >= 10% at <= 5% P&L cost), not as a rule. Size reference: the honest A night has sd 77-97 pts (USD 1,500-1,900 per contract) and the worst honest A night is -243 pts (USD 4,865).

## 10. Limitations and what is not claimed

- Sample size per cell. The vault holds 22 honest A nights before and 17 after the free vetoes (13 under the shadow); the vault effects are carried by 4 (H5) and 1 (H9) A nights, and the shadow's by 10 removed and 1 added. No A-only refinement is testable at this n; the A-average CIs quoted exclude zero through composition, the paired dollar effects on the vault do not.
- The vault is not clean. The forensics (`research/lab/forensics_v0.md`) and the backlog were sized on the full sample including the vault months; every rule here is `effect_seen` under LAB_DESIGN 12.2. Vault-H has been read 41 times and is spent: the Holm family has zero survivors, no test reaches the 0.0083 slot alpha, and the pre-registered 12-slot wallet records none of these as a pass; every later test is Vault-F.
- The discovery effects fail the quarter sign-consistency rule on the A book (Grade A prime 2 of 4 with one untouched quarter; the shadow 2 of 4 on both books, with 2025Q4 explaining its whole discovery gain). A rule that mostly avoids one bad quarter is a regime claim until the forward record says otherwise.
- Top-5 and Sunday dependence remain: 61% of the Grade A prime total sits in five nights (56% of the shadow's); five Sunday nights carry 50% of its vault total; the Sunday band (H8) has not been tested forward, and four Sunday reopens are missing from the feed altogether.
- Hourly bars. First-hit inside a bar is resolved with stop priority; the 15-minute re-resolution (LAB_DESIGN H0h referee measurement) was not run; MFE/MAE are conservative in the exit bar; one same-bar continuation (2026-03-30) swung +133 to -172 on the convention.
- No live record. Fills are the bar open; the 4-pt Sunday and print-night cost is an assumption; the high-cost line is reported everywhere and changes no verdict; nothing is known about fills above one contract (section 9 item 8).
- The detectors and the engine are trained on the same history and the same bars; the detector bridge's null results do not establish independence, and D4 never fires, so it is untested rather than refuted. D3's exit threshold was the tester's choice.
- ES. Untradeable at every grade under every spec (best A average +1.3 ES pts under the superseded S2, +0.1 under Grade A prime); the ES vault arm is uninformative for Grade A prime and contradicts the shadow's A-book effect at the 80% level while supporting its graded book. The replication arm is weak by construction (LAB_DESIGN 12.4).
- Calibration is still off: the mid-band gap of -15 to -20 points on resolved graded nights persists at the honest baseline, and the one second-stage calibrator tried (H1) over-corrects. Months 2025-04..07 of the honest record use raw probabilities (no calibrator yet) and hold 6 of the 62 discovery A nights at +34.9; without them the discovery honest A is +0.1.
- Judgment calls by the testers, kept as they made them: H1's "min 40" read as resolved graded nights; the 20:00 time-stop convention (both reported); the H5 flag evaluated on the builder's post_ret with 2025-09-16 excluded as a splice; the ES 2025-12-16/17 contract toggling flagged, not repaired; the H3 floor on the raw ratio (section 0); the H2 placebo as a derangement over the nights of the trades table.
- Not claimed: that Grade A prime is a confirmed edge (DSR 0.20 vs the 0.95 gate; vault paired effect includes zero); that the geometry layer adds value on the vault (its increment there is -0.4 pts/night); that any exit, checkpoint, detector or cross-asset rule adds value; that the 80 confidence cutoff, the Kospi gate, the scratch cap or the second-stage calibration should be used; that ES should be traded.
