# NQ/ES Asia-session (18:00-03:00 ET) narrative, 2025-11-01 to 2026-01-31

Companion to `catalogue_2025Q4_2026Jan.csv`. All claims below are drawn from the sources cited in the CSV (same-day or next-day Reuters/AP/Bloomberg-syndicated copy, FXEmpire, Investing.com, investingLive, Sharecast/HL, BLS, Federal Reserve, BoJ). Where a magnitude or timing could not be confirmed it is marked **unverified** and repeated in section (c).

**Session convention used.** For Friday RTH dates there is no 18:00 ET Friday Globex session; the "Asia" window for a Friday row is taken to be the next Globex open (Sunday 18:00 ET to Monday 03:00 ET). Dataset rows dated 2025-11-14, 2026-01-09 and 2026-01-23 are read that way. If the dataset's pipeline does something else for Fridays, the Jan 23 row in particular needs re-checking (see unknowns).

## (a) Month-by-month regime narrative

### November 2025 - "AI bubble or not?", data blackout, Japan fiscal/yen stress

**Dominant themes**
- **AI-capex / valuation debate** was the single biggest driver of NQ. Catalysts: Palantir's post-earnings slide despite a beat (Nov 3 after hours), Michael Burry's disclosed NVDA/PLTR puts (Nov 3-4), Goldman/Morgan Stanley/Capital Group CEOs calling 10-20% drawdowns "healthy" at the HKMA summit (Nov 4 HK morning = Nov 3 US evening), SoftBank/Advantest/Tokyo Electron/SK Hynix acting as the high-beta Asia expression of the same trade, and the Nvidia print on Nov 19 (beat-and-raise, +1.7% NQ futures overnight, fully reversed in RTH Nov 20 with the largest intraday reversal since April).
- **US government shutdown** (ended Nov 12) meant no October CPI and a delayed September payrolls report (released Nov 20: +119k, UE 4.4%). The data vacuum amplified Fed-speak: October FOMC minutes (Nov 19) showed division; December-cut odds fell below 40% then recovered above 70% after NY Fed's Williams on Nov 21.
- **Japan**: PM Takaichi's stimulus and Taiwan remarks pushed 30y JGB to 3.31% (Nov 18) and multi-decade highs after the cabinet approved the package (Nov 21); yen sat near a 10-month low (~155-157); China warned citizens against Japan travel (Nov 16-17), hitting Japanese tourism/retail names. Japan Q3 GDP -1.8% annualised (Nov 17).
- **Crypto** sold with AI beta (bitcoin -5.9% on Nov 4, below $90k later in the month).
- Thanksgiving week (Nov 27 holiday, Nov 28 half-day): **unverified** - no same-day market copy retrieved; the only verified weekend catalyst was Ueda's Dec 1 (JST) Nagoya speech pre-announcing a December hike (yen 155.5, 2y JGB 1.01%).

**What Asia did after up vs down RTH days (November)**
- After **down RTH days driven by AI-valuation selling**, Asia continued lower and Tokyo/Seoul amplified the move 2-4x: Nov 3 (-0.30 RTH) -> Asia -1.20; Nov 4 (NQ100 -2.1%) -> Nikkei -2.8%/Kospi -3%; Nov 17 (-0.76) -> Asia -0.83 with Nikkei -3%/Kospi -3.1%; Nov 20 (-2.2% Nasdaq) -> Nikkei -2.4%.
- After **up RTH days**, Asia also continued higher when the up-day had a semis/AI catalyst: Nov 14 (+1.57) -> Sunday-open +0.95 (SK Hynix/Samsung-Nvidia headlines, Kospi +1.9%); Nov 19 (+0.24) -> +0.74 on NVDA, with NQ futures +1.7% at the overnight peak and fading into the US open.
- Net: November was a **momentum/continuation regime** for the overnight session, with Asia ranges of 1.0-1.4% (dataset) and the overnight sign matching the RTH sign in 5 of 5 catalogued November rows.

### December 2025 - Fed cut, "AI capex ROI" angst (Oracle/Broadcom), BoJ hike, year-end thinning

**Dominant themes**
- **FOMC Dec 10**: 25bp cut to 3.50-3.75% with three dissents (Miran for 50bp; Goolsbee and Schmid for hold), projections implying one 2026 cut; the presser read dovish and RTH rallied. The rally was undone overnight by **Oracle** (revenue miss, +$15B capex guide; -11% after hours; Nasdaq-100 futures -1.3% in Asia per Reuters) - AI-capex ROI became the December narrative. **Broadcom** -11% on Dec 12 (AI margin guide) and continued selling Dec 15 (worst day in three weeks) extended it; Micron/AMD/Lam fell in sympathy.
- **Delayed data**: Oct/Nov payrolls on Dec 16 (Nov +64k, Oct -105k, UE 4.6%); Nov CPI on Dec 18 (2.7% y/y, core 2.6%, October not collected) read as soft and triggered a reported rally into **quad witching Dec 19** (CME December roll week was Dec 11-19).
- **BoJ Dec 19** hiked to 0.75% (unanimous, highest since 1995), exactly as pre-announced by Ueda on Dec 1. Outcome was "sell the fact": yen **weakened** after the decision, 10y JGB touched 2%, Nikkei +1% to +1.4% led by chips.
- Christmas/New Year sessions (Dec 24, 26, 31, Jan 2): **unverified** - no same-day copy retrieved.
- Oil: both benchmarks lost >4% in the week to Dec 12 on 2026 surplus expectations; bitcoin fell to $85k (Dec 16).

**What Asia did after up vs down RTH days (December)**
- After the **FOMC up day** (Dec 10, +0.49), Asia **reversed** (-0.89, range 1.60%) because a megacap AI print landed after the close. This is the clearest reversal in the sample and it was earnings-driven, not Fed-driven.
- After the **Dec 15 down day** (-1.17), Asia **continued** (-0.89): Nikkei -1.56% (first close below 50k in 3 months), HSI -1.5%, Kospi -1.6%, NQ futures -0.5%, with the BoJ-hike yen bid and jobs/CPI/BoJ event risk keeping bids away.
- Net: December overnight behaviour was event-dominated; without an after-close catalyst, Asia continued the RTH direction.

### January 2026 - Fed independence, Greenland tariffs, yen 158 -> 154, dollar/gold extremes, megacap earnings

**Dominant themes**
- **Geopolitics/tariffs**: Venezuela/Maduro capture (Sat Jan 3) - muted oil, Nikkei +2.8% on defense names at the Jan 5 open; Greenland: Trump's Saturday Jan 17 threat of 10% (then 25%) tariffs on 8 European countries drove a thin MLK-holiday Globex session (NQ futures -1.1%, Nikkei -1%) and a >2% S&P drop on Tue Jan 20, then a full rebound after Trump dropped the tariff threat/ruled out force (Jan 21-22); Canada: 100% tariff threat if Ottawa signed a China trade deal (weekend Jan 24-25) and 50% on Canadian aircraft (Jan 29-30).
- **Fed independence**: Powell's Sunday Jan 11 video disclosing a DOJ criminal probe (NQ -202 pts at the Globex open); Jan 28 FOMC held at 3.50-3.75% with Miran and Waller dissenting for a cut; reports Jan 29-30 that Warsh (seen as hawkish) was the pick, confirmed Jan 30, after which gold/silver crashed from records (>$5,500 / ~$100).
- **Dollar/yen**: DXY to ~96, near a four-year low (Jan 28); Trump said a weaker dollar was "great". USD/JPY ran to 158.65 (Jan 13, Nikkei +3.1% record on weak yen and Takaichi snap-election/fiscal bets), BoJ held Jan 23 with a hawkish tone (158.6), then intervention-readiness signals from Japanese and US officials knocked USD/JPY to 154.3 by Jan 26 (Nikkei -1.8%, Toyota -4%).
- **Earnings**: Dec CPI in line (2.7%/2.6%) on Jan 13 as JPMorgan kicked off; Intel -17% (Jan 23); ASML record bookings (Jan 28, ~01:00 ET, NQ futures +0.9% by 04:44 ET); MSFT -10% on Azure/capex vs META +10% (Jan 29); Apple beat with a margin warning (Jan 29 after close).
- Kospi crossed 5,000 (Jan 22) and kept printing records; Nikkei >53,000.

**What Asia did after up vs down RTH days (January)**
- After **up RTH days**, Asia was split by catalyst type: when the overnight carried a **semis/dollar** story it continued (Jan 27 +0.33 -> +0.78 on ASML/weak dollar); when a **weekend policy headline** hit the Sunday open it reversed (Jan 9 +0.49 -> -0.71 on DOJ/Powell; Jan 16 -> Greenland). Jan 23 (+0.64 -> dataset +0.92) is unresolved: press reported NQ futures -0.4% early Monday Jan 26 with the yen surging and a Canada tariff threat.
- After the **Jan 29 down day** (-0.77, MSFT), Asia **continued** (-1.12) on Warsh/Canada-tariff/Apple-margin headlines - the only clean January example of after-close megacap + policy risk compounding.
- Net: January overnight sessions were **headline-gap driven** (Sunday opens especially), ranges 0.9-1.2%, with Fed-independence and tariff headlines producing reversals and semis/dollar flows producing continuation.

## (b) Repeating patterns (concrete, testable)

1. **AI-capex-driven RTH selloffs were followed by Asia continuation, with Tokyo/Seoul over-reacting.** Nov 3->4, Nov 4->5, Nov 17->18, Nov 20->21, Dec 15->16: every RTH day where the cited driver was AI valuation/capex saw NQ lower in the 18:00-03:00 window and Nikkei/Kospi down 2-4x the NQ move (SoftBank, Advantest, Tokyo Electron, SK Hynix, Samsung as the proxies). Test: sign(asia_ret) == sign(rth_ret) and |Nikkei| > 2x|NQ asia| when tags contain ai_capex and rth_ret < -0.5.
2. **After-close megacap/AI prints override the RTH direction.** Dec 10 (FOMC rally -> Oracle -> NQ futures -1.3% in Asia), Nov 19 (NVDA -> +1.7% overnight peak then faded), Jan 28/29 (MSFT/META split, then Apple + Warsh). When a $100B+ AI-exposed company reports after 16:00 ET, the overnight sign follows the print's capex/margin read, not the RTH tape. Test: rows tagged earnings_megacap/earnings_semis with a post-close report show |asia_ret| > 0.7 and sign independent of rth_ret.
3. **The post-NVDA overnight peak was not held.** Nov 19-20: NQ futures +1.7% at ~21:00 ET, RTH closed -2.2%. Nov 21 Asia then sold the Japan AI proxies (Advantest -12%). Test: overnight high after NVDA earnings vs next RTH close.
4. **Sunday 18:00 ET opens in January gapped on weekend policy headlines regardless of the Friday RTH sign.** Jan 11 (DOJ/Powell, NQ -202 pts), Jan 18 (Greenland, NQ -1.1% in MLK-thin trade), Jan 25 (yen intervention signals + Canada tariff; press: NQ -0.4% early). All three Friday RTH sessions had been up days. Test: Friday rth_ret is uninformative for Sunday-open asia_ret in Jan 2026; headline tags (fed/tariffs/geopolitics) dominate.
5. **Semis prints inside the 18:00-03:00 window are clean NQ catalysts.** Samsung/SK Hynix-Nvidia headlines (Nov 16-17, NQ +0.7%), Micron into BoJ day (Dec 17-19), ASML at ~01:00 ET (Jan 28, NQ +0.9% by 04:44 ET). Test: NQ asia_ret > +0.6 on nights with a positive semis print in Asia/Europe hours.
6. **BoJ: fully-priced hikes produced "sell the fact" in yen and "buy the fact" in Nikkei; hawkish surprises came from speeches, not meetings.** Dec 19 hike (unanimous, priced ~80% since Ueda's Dec 1 speech): yen weakened, Nikkei +1-1.4%, 10y JGB 2%. Jan 23 hold with hawkish projections: yen flat at 158.6, Nikkei +0.3%. The yen shocks that hurt Nikkei were Ueda's Dec 1 Nagoya speech (155.5) and the Jan 24-26 intervention signalling (158 -> 154, Nikkei -1.8%). Test: |USDJPY move| on BoJ decision days < |USDJPY move| on Ueda-speech / MoF-signal days in this window.
7. **Weak yen = Nikkei records = cushioned NQ overnight.** Jan 12-13 (USD/JPY 158.65, Nikkei +3.1% record; FXEmpire: yen slide "cushions US futures") and Nov 20-21 (yen at 10-month low did not prevent Nikkei -2.4%) show the cushion only works absent an AI-valuation shock. Test: on nights with USD/JPY up > 0.5%, asia_ret for NQ is less negative than on yen-up nights, conditional on no ai_capex tag.
8. **Pre-CPI nights were NOT compressed in this window.** Jan 12->13: Nikkei +3.1% record; Dec 17->18: Micron print and BoJ positioning. The dataset's Asia ranges on these nights should be checked, but the press record shows active, catalyst-driven sessions rather than quiet ones.
9. **FOMC nights: the Fed itself was not the overnight driver.** Dec 10 (Oracle) and Jan 28 (MSFT/META, gold +4%) both had larger non-Fed catalysts. Test: asia_rng on FOMC nights (1.60 on Dec 10) exceeds the window median, but attribution is to earnings/dollar, not to the statement.
10. **Thin-holiday Globex sessions amplify headline moves.** MLK (Jan 19): NQ futures -1.1% on Greenland with US cash/Treasuries closed. Test: holiday_thin + tariffs/geopolitics rows show |asia_ret| above the window median.
11. **Technical-level breaks were followed through overnight.** Nov 17 close below the 50-DMA (first since April) -> Nikkei -3%/NQ -0.83 overnight; Dec 15 Nikkei first close below 50,000 followed the Dec 12-15 Broadcom/Oracle break. Test: rows tagged technical_level with negative rth_ret have negative asia_ret.
12. **Dollar-slide nights were NQ-positive, gold-positive.** Jan 27->28 (DXY 96.0, gold +4.1%, NQ +0.9%), Dec 10->11 (dollar down on Fed message but Oracle dominated). Test: on nights with DXY -0.2% or worse and no earnings shock, NQ asia_ret > 0.
