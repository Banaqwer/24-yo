## (b) Repeating patterns — concrete, testable statements (sample = the 16 user rows + CSV extras)

1. **Capitulation reversal.** After an NQ RTH loss ≥ 2%, the Asia session closed *up* with range ≥ 1%: 2/2 (Oct 16 → +0.85/1.06; Nov 5 → +1.28/2.98). Mechanism both times: forced selling at the Seoul/Tokyo open (halts, record HF tech sales) then US futures bid. Test: condition on Kospi circuit-breaker/sidecar triggered within first 30 min and NQ ≤ -2%; expect positive 18:00→03:00 return.
2. **Moderate-down-day continuation.** After NQ RTH between -0.3% and -1.3% with an AI/rates driver, Asia continued lower: 5/6 (Aug 17, Sep 23, Oct 12, Oct 14, Nov 6). The exception (Sep 16) was an FOMC day. Test: exclude FOMC/quarter-end days; expect negative overnight.
3. **Regime flip on up days (late Sep onward).** Through Sep 18, Asia after an NQ up day continued higher (3/3: Aug 4, Sep 17, Sep 18). From Sep 25, Asia faded every NQ up day (4/4: Sep 25, Oct 21, Oct 23, Nov 4), average -0.99%. Common driver: Korea/Japan semis selling (Kospi -2.7/-3.5/-1.5/-5.3%) plus an oil/Iran headline. Test: "fade the US close in Asia" worked only once Kospi was in its drawdown from the June 9,385 peak (Oct 15: -41%).
4. **Event-day reversals.** FOMC (Sep 16: -0.49 → +0.68) and quarter-end (Sep 30: -0.07 → +1.03) both reversed the US session; Oct 28 FOMC hold was flat RTH but Alphabet AH drove the overnight. Test: on FOMC days, overnight sign ≠ RTH sign.
5. **Weekend gaps are geopolitics-first, then idiosyncratic.** Sunday 18:00 opens: Sep 27 -0.2% (Trump rejected Iran deal), Oct 18 +0.4% (Iran threats but oversold), Oct 25 +0.6% (strike leaks, oil off), Nov 8 -0.8% (Nvidia probe). Friday-row Asia returns: +0.65, -0.64, +0.85, -0.71, -1.06 — sign matched the weekend's *oil* direction in 4/5 (Sep 20 oil down/NQ up; Sep 27 oil up/NQ down; Oct 18 oil down/NQ up; Oct 25 oil down but NQ down — exception; Nov 8 n/a). Test: overnight NQ sign vs Sunday-night Brent sign.
6. **Range expansion is calendar-bound.** Asia ranges ≥ 1.5% occurred only on quarter-end + semis earnings (Sep 30: 1.52), megacap earnings + Iran headline (Oct 21: 1.64), election week (Nov 3-5: 1.58, 2.98) and the Nvidia weekend (Nov 6: 2.09). Ordinary Fed/CPI nights ranged 0.7–1.3%.
7. **Seoul open is the overnight pivot (post-Oct 12).** Kospi sidecar/halt timestamps: Oct 12 20:53 ET (down), Oct 21 ~20:00 ET (up), Oct 27 (up, +6.6%), Nov 6 19:03 ET (down), Nov 10 (up, +5%). Test: NQ move in the 30 min after Seoul open explains most of asia_ret since mid-Oct.
8. **Hot US data = NQ down, soft = up, but Asia doesn't always follow.** NFP Sep 4 (hot) and CPI Oct 14 (hot) were down days; NFP Aug 7 and Oct 2 (soft) up days. CPI Oct 14's overnight continued (-1.18) only because it stacked on AI-credit news.
9. **BoJ hikes have been yen-negative and Nikkei-positive** (Sep 18: 7-2 split read dovish; yen >157; Nikkei +1.4%), so BoJ-day Asia sessions were *not* NQ-negative (Sep 17 row +0.65). Oct 30 hold with "2% achieved" language + ETF-sale acceleration coincided with Nikkei -2.1% but the dominant driver was Amazon/Alphabet.
10. **Dollar barely matters; yen matters at the extremes.** DXY stayed 100–102.4 the whole window. Yen moves >0.5% in the US session coincided with risk-off (Nov 5 yen +0.5%, Nov 9 +0.5%) — a yen bid on equity down days is the carry-unwind tell to monitor at 158.5 (Nov 9 intraday high).
