# NQ/ES Asia-session (18:00–03:00 ET) narrative, May–July 2026

Companion to `catalogue_2026Q2_Jul.csv`. Everything below is sourced from same-/next-day press (CNBC live blogs, TheStreet, Yahoo/Bloomberg, Investing.com, investingLive, Nikkei Asia, BoJ/Fed coverage); URLs are in the CSV `sources` column. Where I could not verify something I say so in section (c).

Conventions: "Asia" = the Globex session after the listed RTH date. Friday rows in the sample (May 29, Jun 5, Jul 10) can only be the **Sunday-evening** session (Globex is closed Fri 17:00–Sun 18:00), and the data behave that way (e.g. Jun 5 row = Jun 7/8 session). Jul 2 is a Thursday-night session into the Jul 3 US holiday (Globex open, early close).

## (a) Month-by-month regime

### Backdrop that runs through the whole window
- **US–Iran war** (started Feb 28, 2026; Khamenei killed; Hormuz restricted; Apr 7 ceasefire; US naval blockade Apr 13 → mid-June). Every overnight session had a Hormuz/oil headline risk. Sequence inside the window: May 7 Hormuz exchange of fire ("love tap"); May 11 Trump rejects Iran counter-proposal (WTI > $100); May 28 Axios 60-day memorandum; Jun 7 Israel–Iran strikes; Jun 10 US strikes then Trump cancels further strikes same evening; Jun 14 Pakistan-mediated framework; **Jun 17 14-point MoU signed (Versailles)**; Jun 21 US strikes on nuclear sites / Iran shuts its part of Hormuz; Jun 28 ceasefire resumed; **Jul 8 ceasefire collapses** (Iran hits shipping, US strikes resume nightly); Jul 11–13 Iran declares Hormuz closed, US re-blockades, 20% transit fee (dropped Jul 14); Jul 23 Brent > $100 (Houthi tanker attacks); Jul 26 US pauses bombing, Brent −8.7% on Jul 27.
- **Fed regime change.** Kevin Warsh's first FOMC was Jun 16–17: hold at 3.50–3.75% but dots flipped to a 2026 hike (median 3.8%), easing bias dropped. Jul 28–29: hold again, **9–3 with three hawkish dissents**, 30y > 5.2% (highest since 2007). Market moved from "cuts" (May) to "hikes" (June) to "credibility" (July).
- **BoJ:** Jun 16 hike to 1.00% (highest since 1995, energy pass-through) landed in Asia hours; Jul 31 hold at 1.00% (8–1) with MoF intervention the same day. USD/JPY: ~160 in June, 163.15 four-decade low on Jul 22, −2.6% in one Asian morning on Jul 30, ~158 by Jul 31.
- **AI/semis positioning** is the dominant cross-asset channel into the Asia session: Kospi (Samsung + SK Hynix > 40–50% of index) moved ±5–18% on 10 separate days in the window, with circuit breakers on Jun 8, Jun 23, Jun 26, Jul 7, Jul 28 and a sidecar on Jul 20/Jul 3/Jul 15/Jul 31. SK Hynix overtook Samsung as Korea's largest cap on Jun 22, listed ADRs on Jul 10, and fell 15% in Seoul on Jul 13.

### May 2026 — melt-up with geopolitical chop (regime: "buy the dip, oil is noise")
- Records almost daily: S&P > 7,500 (May 14), Nasdaq +8% in May; Nikkei +5% to 62,834 record on May 7; Kospi > 8,000 (May 15 intraday), 8,788 (Jun 1). AI capex / memory (Micron, SK Hynix, Samsung HBM) were the leadership. Nvidia reported **May 20** (not May 27): beat, $80B buyback, muted reaction.
- Inflation was already hot (April CPI 3.8% y/y on May 12; 30y 5.11% on May 15; Dec hike odds 51%) but was shrugged off except on May 15 (Nasdaq −1.5%).
- **Asia after up days:** mild give-back when a geopolitical/rates headline landed (May 11 → −0.61% on Trump rejecting Iran's offer + oil +4%; May 14 → −0.96% on underwhelming Trump–Xi summit, Samsung strike, 30y 5.11%). **Asia after down days:** bought (May 7 → +0.89% as "love tap" de-escalation; May 27 → −0.70% is the exception: fresh US strikes in Iran, missile at Kuwait). Sunday May 31: Iran "shuts Hormuz" + missiles at Kuwait yet NQ +0.66% — the market had learned to fade Iran escalation headlines.

### June 2026 — the stress episode (regime: "rates up + AI de-rating, Asia is the amplifier")
What the shock was: **not one thing — a sequence that compounded.**
1. **Jun 3–5: earnings + data.** Broadcom (Jun 3 AH) beat but kept AI guidance unchanged → AVGO −12.5%, Micron −8% (Jun 4). Then May payrolls +172k vs ~80k (Jun 5) flipped Fed pricing to hikes; with a reported Meta secondary and a skeptical AI note on the tape, Nasdaq −4.18%, NDX ≈ −5%, SOXX −10% (worst in 6+ years), ~$1T of chip cap gone. This is the **Jun 5 RTH −3.45%** row.
2. **Jun 7–10: geopolitics + CPI.** Weekend Israel–Iran strikes; Kospi −8% (circuit breaker) and Nikkei −3.85% on Jun 8 catching down to the US, while NQ futures were bought (+0.70% Asia). May CPI 4.2% y/y (3-yr high) on Jun 10 plus US "self-defense strikes" and Trump's "hit Iran VERY HARD TONIGHT" → Nasdaq −1.98%; **Trump cancelled the strikes that evening** → NQ +1.18% overnight, Nasdaq +2.54% Jun 11. SpaceX IPO priced $135 (Jun 11), debuted +19% (Jun 12) — a liquidity sink that several strategists blamed for AI-trade selling.
3. **Jun 16–17: central banks.** BoJ hike to 1% (Jun 16 Asia hours), Warsh's hawkish-dot FOMC (Jun 17): S&P −1.21%, 2y +16bp. Yet the Asia sessions were **positive both nights** (+0.79%, +0.92%) because the Iran MoU was being signed and Nikkei/Kospi were printing records (71,000 / 9,000). The hawkish Fed was absorbed by Asia as a dollar/yen story, not an equity story.
4. **Jun 18 (quad witching pulled forward; Jun 19 Juneteenth)**: SpaceX peaked ($185), Nikkei/Kospi record closes. Overnight −0.76% has no identified catalyst (see unknowns).
5. **Jun 22→23: the −2.26% Asia session.** Jun 22 RTH was a megacap story (Alphabet −5% on AI-talent exits, AMZN −4.8%, SpaceX −16%, 10y 4.50%) while Micron rose 7% and Asia had opened higher on Vance's "talks progressing". The reversal started ~22:30 ET in Seoul: Kospi −4% → closed −10% (record point drop, two circuit breakers), Samsung and SK Hynix each −12%+ the day after Hynix overtook Samsung; Nikkei −3.55% ended an eight-day streak; NQ −2.7% and SOXX −5.9% by 05:55 ET; Europe tech −3%. **No single headline caused it.** Korean coverage/analysts attribute it to forced liquidation of leveraged semiconductor ETFs, a pre-session FSC warning on overheated tech and leveraged ETFs, profit-taking/deleveraging after a ~100% Kospi run, SK Hynix's same-day disclosure that it would slow HBM4 conversion in favour of DDR5 profits, and Warsh-hike worries. Bloomberg/WaPo frame it as "AI chip valuation fears" and "signals of higher rates + AI spending concern". The Jun 23 RTH follow-through (Nasdaq −2.21%, SMH −7%, Micron −13% ahead of earnings) confirms it was positioning, not fundamentals: Micron then beat massively on Jun 24 ($41.5B rev, $50B guide) and rose 16% — and the Nasdaq still fell (rotation out of megacaps, Apple −6% on memory-cost warning).
6. **Jun 25→26: OpenAI IPO delay** (NYT) hit the AI complex in Asia — SoftBank −11–13% intraday, Kospi −5.8% (second circuit breaker that week), Nikkei −4.15%; NQ −0.80% into quarter-end trimming. Nasdaq fell five straight sessions through Jun 26 (week −4.6%); health care +7% that week (best since 2022) — the "Great Rotation".
7. **How it ended (first leg):** no catalyst, just exhaustion plus the Jun 28 ceasefire resumption and quarter-end. Nasdaq rebounded from 25,477 (Jun 24) to 26,040 (Jul 1) and 26,282 (Jul 10) while the Dow and Russell made records. H1 stats: Nasdaq +12.8%, Russell +22%, chips +80%.

Propagation through Asia hours, mechanically: US chip close → Kospi open (20:00 ET) is the transmission line. Every large NQ Asia move in June coincided with a ≥5% Kospi day (Jun 5/8, 23, 26) and a ≥3% Nikkei day (SoftBank/Advantest/Tokyo Electron as the Japanese AI proxies). The yen was a sideshow for NQ (USD/JPY pinned ~160 by intervention and the BoJ hike) — the **yen-carry channel did not fire in June**; it was pure equity-positioning contagion. Oil was anti-correlated with the stress: Brent fell from ~$96 (early June) to < $75 (Jun 26) as the MoU took hold, so June's selloff was *not* an oil shock.

### July 2026 — second leg: oil + rates + capex + China (regime: "Nasdaq-100 −7%, Dow records")
- The relief rally ended Jul 1–2 with semis profit-taking (SMH −4.5% on Jul 2 despite a weak NFP of +57k) and Samsung's "sell the news" record guidance (Jul 7, Kospi −4.9%). Jul 8 the ceasefire collapsed; oil climbed from $69 to $100 (Jul 23) and the 10y from 4.5% to 4.7%+. TSMC's capex raise (Jul 16), Alphabet's $205B capex (Jul 22) and Tesla's record −15% earnings day (Jul 23) reset the "AI capex = bad for equity holders" narrative. CXMT's +470% Shanghai debut and a China-DUV-lithography report (Jul 27) added a China-competition leg: Samsung −13.4% (worst in ~20 years), SK Hynix −14.7%, Kospi −10.8% (Jul 28).
- Nasdaq fell six straight days into the Jul 29 FOMC (three hawkish dissents, 30y > 5.2%, Dow −1,100). **The clearing event was Jul 30**: Microsoft (Azure +43%, +16%) and the reported forced liquidation of Situational Awareness's public book; JPM said hedge-fund chip deleveraging was complete; SOXX +8%, Kospi +17.9% (best day ever) on Jul 31 with BoJ hold + MoF intervention. July: NDX −6.95% (worst since Mar 2025), SMH −16.9% (worst month since 2008), SpaceX −36%; Dow +0.3%.
- **Asia after up days in July:** gave back when the "up" was a gap-and-stall (Jul 6 → −0.91%, Jul 10 → −0.88% Sunday, Jul 21 → −0.74%). **Asia after down days:** bounced (Jul 2 → +0.68%, Jul 8 → +0.68%, Jul 14 → +0.75%, Jul 20 → +1.28%, Jul 29 → +0.82%) except when the down day was itself an Asia-origin chip rout that continued (Jul 16 → −1.21%, Jul 27 → −0.93%).

## (b) Repeating patterns — concrete, testable statements

1. **Mean reversion dominates after US-origin shocks; continuation dominates after Asia-origin shocks.** Of the 24 sample sessions, sign(asia) = −sign(rth) in 15. The 9 continuations are almost all nights where the catalyst was *Asian* (Jun 4 Kospi −5.5%; Jun 25 Kospi −5.8%; Jul 16 Japan AI names −9%; Jul 27 Kospi −10.8%) or the RTH move was a record-high grind (May 29, Jun 8, Jul 8, Jul 14). Test: condition on whether Kospi's close-to-close move exceeds ±3%; expect continuation when it does and reversal when it does not.
2. **RTH ≤ −1.5% → Asia positive.** Jun 5 (−3.45 → +0.70), Jun 16 (−1.67 → +0.79), Jun 17 (−1.63 → +0.92), Jul 2 (−2.14 → +0.68), Jul 29 (−2.04 → +0.82) — 5 of 6; the exception Jun 25 (−1.50 → −0.80) had a fresh after-hours catalyst (OpenAI IPO delay). Test: after a ≥1.5% NQ RTH drop with no new AH headline, long at 18:00 / flat at 03:00.
3. **Gap-and-stall RTH days (|rth| < 0.1% open-to-close after a big overnight gap up) are followed by negative Asia sessions**: Jun 8 (+0.64 is the counter-example), Jul 6 (−0.91), Jul 14 (+0.75 counter-example), Jul 10 (−0.88). Mixed — only 2 of 4; drop unless more data.
4. **Fed/BoJ decision nights are not the big Asia nights.** FOMC Jun 17 → +0.92; FOMC Jul 29 → +0.82; BoJ Jun 16 (Asia hours) and Jul 31 (Asia hours) coincided with Nikkei records / +4%. The hawkish-Fed reaction was absorbed in RTH; Asia bought it. Test: Asia range on FOMC night < sample median (Jun 17 rng 1.14 and Jul 29 rng 1.82 vs median ~1.3 — only partly true; range is average, direction is positive).
5. **CPI nights (Jun 10, Jul 14) were both positive in Asia (+1.18, +0.75)** regardless of the print's sign — because the overnight driver was a *geopolitical de-escalation* (Trump cancelling strikes; Trump dropping the Hormuz fee). Test: CPI-day Asia direction is explained by the 18:00–22:00 ET Iran headline, not by CPI.
6. **NFP nights: Jun 5 (hot) → Sunday +0.70; Jul 2 (weak) → +0.68.** Both positive, both after a ≥2% RTH drop. Same as pattern 2.
7. **Kospi open (20:00 ET) is the inflection.** On Jun 22/23, Jul 12/13, Jul 6/7 and Jul 27/28 the NQ move was flat at 18:00–20:00 and almost entirely accrued 20:00–03:00. Test: split the window at 20:00 ET; the 20:00–03:00 sub-session should carry > 80% of |asia_ret| on days where Kospi moved ≥ 5%.
8. **Oil was NOT the equity driver overnight except via the Fed-hike channel.** Brent spikes (May 11 +4%, Jul 13 +4%, Jul 21 +3.4%) coincided with negative NQ Asia sessions only when they came with rate-hike repricing; oil *drops* (Jun 22, Jul 27 −8.7%) did not help NQ when a chip catalyst was present. Test: regress asia_ret on Δoil and ΔKospi; expect the Kospi coefficient to dominate.
9. **Yen-carry did not fire.** USD/JPY stayed 158–163 all window; the one yen shock (Jul 30, −2.6% in Asian morning) coincided with a *positive* NQ session (+0.82). Tag `yen_carry` is informational, not a direction predictor in this window.
10. **Largest ranges (≥ 1.8%) cluster around Asia circuit-breaker days**: Jun 10 (1.89), Jun 22 (2.45), Jun 25 (2.46), Jul 29 (1.82). Test: Korean circuit breaker / sidecar flag ⇒ asia_rng above 75th percentile.
11. **Thin-holiday sessions were small and negative:** Jun 18→19 (Juneteenth, −0.76, rng 0.86), Jul 2→3 (+0.68 but rng 0.86). Sample too small; keep as a prior only.
12. **The "RTH driver" was frequently a rotation, not an index shock**: Dow records on Jun 4, Jun 16, Jun 22 (Russell), Jul 2, Jul 6, Jul 28 all came with Nasdaq down. NQ-specific overnight behaviour therefore tracked SOX/SMH and Kospi far better than ES. Test: asia_ret(NQ) − asia_ret(ES) spread should be explained by Kospi/SMH, not by S&P.

## (c) Unknowns / could not verify

- **Jun 18→19 overnight (−0.76%)**: no dated Asia wrap found for Fri Jun 19 (Juneteenth). I backed out Nikkei ≈ 71,250 (+0.3%) and Kospi ≈ 9,052 (−0.1%) from the Jun 22 closes and percentage moves — an inference, not a report. Driver tagged `no_news`.
- **Jun 22→23 exact trigger**: the 22:30 ET Kospi reversal has no single attributable headline in the English-language press I could reach (Bloomberg and WaPo pages were paywalled/403; Korean-press detail came via TechTimes/KED). The forced-liquidation-of-leveraged-ETFs explanation is the most specific but is second-hand.
- **Broadcom report date**: CNBC says Wednesday night Jun 3; some aggregator pieces say Jun 4 after hours. I used Jun 3 AH / Jun 4 reaction (consistent with CNBC's Jun 4 RTH AVGO −12.5%).
- **June roll date**: CME roll-dates page returned 503 twice. Jun 11 (Thu) roll and Jun 18 expiry are inferred from the CME convention (roll 8 days before expiry; expiry moved off the Jun 19 holiday). Not independently confirmed.
- **Meta secondary offering (Jun 5)** and the "skeptical AI report" are mentioned by TheStreet/Yahoo headlines only; no primary detail retrieved.
- **Bank of Korea hike around Jul 16** appears in one search summary; not confirmed from a BoK source.
- **Nvidia May 20 after-hours move**: sources conflict (Zacks "surges" vs Kiplinger "closes lower"); the extra row flags this.
- **Index closing levels** in the CSV are as reported by live blogs and occasionally conflict by a few points across outlets; percentages are more reliable than levels. Some CNBC Kospi levels (e.g. Jul 13 "8,806") are internally inconsistent with adjacent days; I quoted percent moves where levels looked wrong.
- **WebSearch budget** for this session was exhausted before Jul 27–31 and the "end of selloff" searches; those dates were filled from CNBC live-blog fetches only (good coverage, single outlet).
- **Why Asia opened higher on Jun 22/23 before reversing**: Vance's comments are cited by CNBC; whether a specific Korean regulatory statement (FSC) hit intraday is asserted by TechTimes but not time-stamped.
