# Signal Register

Living register for the Alt-Data Edge Research Pipeline. One row per signal, updated at the end of every phase.

Status vocabulary: IDEA, PARKED, SOURCING, SPECIFIED, AWAITING DATA, TESTING, VALIDATED, PROMISING, INCONCLUSIVE, REJECTED.
Category vocabulary: Physical, Regulatory, Digital, Second-order.
Scores: Crowdedness 1 = heavily sold/published, 5 = nobody covers it. Signal/noise 1 = weak proxy, 5 = direct physical measure. Legal risk 1 = clearly public and licensed, 5 = stop.
